Privacy Policy

Lekto: Text to Speech Reader · Last updated September 26, 2026

Lekto reads text aloud with the voices built into your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your texts, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Texts you paste, the PDFs and files you open, articles you fetch by link and the text found in photos of pages.
  • Your shelf, the place you stopped in each text, your chosen voice and speed.
  • Your onboarding answers (what you read and why you listen).

All of this is stored only in Lekto's own storage on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Voices and Personal Voice

Speech is produced by Apple's on-device speech synthesis. If you allow it, Lekto can read with the Personal Voice you created in iOS Settings. Lekto does not record, copy or upload your voice; iOS keeps it on your device.

Photos and camera

When you read a page from a photo, the text is recognised on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. Photos are not uploaded or stored by Lekto.

Links

When you read an article by link, Lekto downloads that web page directly from the site you entered, the same way a browser does. We don't see which links you open.

Audio files

Audio files you export are created on your iPhone and shared only where you choose to share them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Lekto receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate purchases and measure how the paywall performs; it never receives your texts.

Analytics and tracking

Lekto does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above.

Deleting your data

Remove texts from your shelf with the × next to each one, or delete Lekto from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

Children

Lekto is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

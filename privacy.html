Privacy

Last updated October 6, 2026

What you read in Lekto is yours. It is stored on your iPhone and read aloud by your iPhone.

What Lekto keeps, and where

The texts you paste, the PDFs and files you open, articles you add by link, text found in photographed pages, your shelf, the exact word where you stopped, your chosen voice and speed, and your answers to the first-run questions. All of it sits in Lekto’s own storage on the device. None of it is sent to us.

How the speaking works

Speech is made by Apple’s speech synthesis on the iPhone. If you allow it, Lekto can read in the Personal Voice you made in iOS Settings; iOS keeps that voice on the device and Lekto neither records nor copies it. Saved audio files are created on the phone and go only where you share them.

Photographed pages

The camera is used to photograph a printed page. The words are recognised on the iPhone with Apple’s Vision framework. The picture is not uploaded and is not stored by Lekto.

Articles by link

When you add a web article, Lekto downloads that page straight from the address you typed or shared, the way a browser would, and keeps the readable text. The site you named can see the request, as with any visit. We do not see which links you open.

Purchases

Apple handles payment for the subscriptions and the one-time purchase, and we never see your card. Lekto uses the subscription service Adapty, which receives an anonymous install ID and your purchase status to unlock Pro and to measure how the purchase screen performs. It never receives your texts.

Notifications

Any reminder Lekto shows is a local notification set by iOS on your phone. No push server is involved.

What Lekto leaves out

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising identifier and no tracking across other apps or sites.
  • No analytics kit apart from the subscription service above.

Deleting

Remove a text from the shelf with the × next to it, or delete Lekto to remove everything it stored. iOS may include app storage in your iCloud or computer backup if you use those; that is controlled in iOS Settings.

Children, changes, contact

Lekto is not made for children under 13. A revised page carries a new date. Questions go to netkaktakto@gmail.com.

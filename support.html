Common questions

  • Voices sound robotic. Download free Enhanced or Premium voices in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices; they appear in Lekto right away.
  • Personal Voice isn't listed. Create one in Settings › Accessibility › Personal Voice (iOS 17+, recent iPhones), then allow Lekto to use it.
  • A PDF has no text. Scanned PDFs are pictures. Take a photo of the page instead — Lekto reads text from photos.
  • Restore a purchase. Open Lekto › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Cancel. Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Lekto.

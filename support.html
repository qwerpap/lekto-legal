Support

Last updated October 6, 2026

Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com. A person replies, usually within two working days.

The voice sounds robotic

Download an Enhanced or Premium voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. It appears in Lekto straight away.

My Personal Voice is missing

Create it in Settings → Accessibility → Personal Voice (iOS 17 or later, on a supported iPhone), then allow Lekto to use it.

A PDF opens with no text

Scanned PDFs are pictures of pages. Photograph the page in Lekto instead; it reads text from photos.

An article link reads only part of the page

Lekto keeps the readable story and drops menus and ads. If a site hides the text behind a login, copy the text and paste it in.

Restore a purchase

Lekto → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID that bought it.

Cancel a subscription

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lekto.
